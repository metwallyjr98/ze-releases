Questions

Do I need to pay for AI tokens?

No. Transcription and AI editing run locally on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

How do I install it?

Unzip, double-click “Install ZE”, restart Premiere and open Window › Extensions › ZE. The panel downloads its engines for you on first run.

My Mac says it “couldn’t verify” the installer

Press Done, open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click “Open Anyway” next to Install ZE, then open it again. This only happens the first time.

Can I move my licence to a new computer?

Yes. Open ⚙ Settings › Deactivate on the old one, then activate on the new one.

Which Premiere version do I need?

Premiere Pro 2023 (v23) or newer, on macOS or Windows 10/11.
